# What's new in each version: shown in the app after an update, and on the download page.
# Plain words about what's improved; no dates or times. Newest first.

## 1.5.0
- New: Alerts on other phones. Get Always-on mode's alerts on your everyday Android phone, with the same beeps. Scan a code once; no accounts, no extra apps.
- Your other phone's home screen shows what's on now, and tells you if the spare phone goes quiet (off, unplugged, or no internet).
- Your other phone also shows the spare phone's kahraba pattern, history and whole switch log, kept up to date automatically. At the top of Your power, choose whose data to see.
- Alerts are encrypted. If the internet is down, they wait and go as soon as it's back.
- Give the spare phone a clear name, like Kitchen phone, so your other phones show “From Kitchen phone”.

## 1.4.10
- Every alert now comes as its own notification, so apps that pass alerts on to your other phones never miss one.
- Kahraba is announced sooner when the light jumps up and then settles a little.
- The light graph shows kahraba in green from when it actually came on.
- New “What it learned…” button under Room on the Always-on screen: remove a single “It's kahraba” or “It's moteur” teaching, for example one saved to the wrong room.

## 1.4.09
- Always-on mode now also spots kahraba in the daytime when it's barely brighter than the moteur: a light that stays almost perfectly still for 45 minutes. It still shows the time kahraba came on.

## 1.4.08
- Always-on mode tells you when the light is switched off but the charger is still on, so you can switch it back on. It's not mistaken for a power cut.
- Kahraba alerts come 2 minutes sooner: the app now checks the light for 3 minutes instead of 5.

## 1.4.07
- The Always-on light graph has more views: 30 seconds, 1, 3, 5 and 10 minutes, 1 hour, 6 hours and 24 hours. It's also a little shorter.
- “It's kahraba” and “It's moteur” are now near the top of the Always-on screen, no scrolling needed.
- New in Settings: Text size. Choose Smaller, Default or Larger for the text in the app.

## 1.4.06
- The Always-on light graph is now part of Live readings, with a new 30-second view that updates every second.

## 1.4.05
- Checks show the light level, the wobble and a live graph again, and the result keeps them with a graph of the whole check.
- Always-on mode has a light graph: the last 10 minutes, hour, 6 hours or 24 hours, shaded green for kahraba, orange for moteur and grey for power off.

## 1.4.04
- The menu shows the app's name and version at the top, and has Check for updates.
- Clearer icons for rooms and feedback.

## 1.4.03
- The top-right button is now a menu (☰): Always-on mode, the Light meter, the Amps calculator, rooms and all checks, with Settings and Help at the bottom.

## 1.4.02
- Choose your room right from the home screen: tap the room at the top.

## 1.4.01
- Your power is now a sheet that slides up over the home screen: the kahraba pattern, recent checks and accuracy. More cards can be turned on in Settings › Cards.
- The gear opens Tools: Always-on mode, the Light meter, the Amps calculator, rooms, all checks, settings and help.

## 1.4.0
- A calmer home screen: one big Check button, what's on now, and when kahraba may come next.
- Press and hold the button, or tap the Always-on chip, for Always-on mode.
- Swipe up for Your power: your room, the kahraba pattern, history and all your checks.
- Check results fill the screen, with Correct and Incorrect buttons.

## 1.3.02
- The Kahraba pattern card has a clearer colour key for its bars.
- Always-on mode says clearly what it uses: only the light sensor and the charger. No camera, microphone or internet.
- Cards start in a more useful order: What to run, Kahraba pattern, Recent checks, History, Tools. If you arranged your own, it's kept (Settings › Cards can reset it).

## 1.3.01
- The Kahraba pattern card now sums up today and the last 7 days in plain words: how long kahraba was on, when, and how many power cuts.
- Days when Always-on mode was off now show blank on the bars.

## 1.3.0
- New Kahraba pattern card: the last 7 days at a glance, how long kahraba usually lasts, and when it may come next. It learns from Always-on mode, so keep it running.

## 1.2.05
- Fewer false “Kahraba is back” alerts: the app checks the light stays still for 5 minutes before it beeps, and shows the time kahraba actually came on.

## 1.2.04
- Starting Always-on mode now asks which light the phone is under: halogen, LED, other light, or power cuts only.

## 1.2.03
- Always-on mode can now just log power cuts, for a phone under an LED or with no lamp. Choose it when you start, or turn on “Power cuts only”.

## 1.2.02
- Times now show am/pm, like 8:09 PM, in Recent checks, All checks and the Always-on mode log.

## 1.2.01
- A “What's new” window, like this one, after each update.

## 1.2.0
- Always-on mode catches kahraba when a busy moteur suddenly goes still and the light gets brighter, even when the change comes in small steps or after a power cut.
- If the phone falls off its stand, it beeps and says “Phone moved – please check” instead of “Power off”.
- When the power comes back after an outage, the alert has “It's kahraba” and “It's moteur” buttons.
- Beeps are now a higher, kitchen-timer pitch.

## 1.1.04
- Always-on mode catches kahraba coming back after a short power blink.
- “Always-on monitor” is now called “Always-on mode”.

## 1.1.03
- Fewer false “Kahraba is back” alerts.
